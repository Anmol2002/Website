01Overview

An animated hero for a global beauty store, built frame by frame from a blank page to a product promo.

HOK is a beauty store bringing global makeup, skincare and fragrance to India. I designed and animated an eleven-frame hero sequence in Figma: logo, headline build, navigation, a rising product scene, floating category tags and a promo reveal for a viral gloss stick.

11animated frames
4category tags
1promo reveal
1asset kit

02The challenge

What had to be solved

Beauty store homepages are crowded with offers. HOK needed a first impression that feels luxurious, then still drives to a product.

Design goals

  1. Build the headline with rhythm
  2. Make products rise into view
  3. Introduce categories without a menu
  4. Lead into a product promo

03Process

How I approached it

  1. Blank to brandThe logo appears first, then the headline builds line by line.
  2. Scene riseProducts and flowers rise into the hero.
  3. Floating tagsCategory tags drift in and settle.
  4. Promo revealScrolling reveals a full-width product promo.

04Headline build

A headline that builds.

Blank, logo, first line, second line, then navigation.

05Scene and tags

Products rise, categories float.

The product scene rises and category tags drift into place.

06Promo reveal

India's viral gloss stick.

A scroll reveals the promo banner with price and call to action.

07Asset kit

Everything needed to build it.

Product cut-outs and imagery prepared as a kit.

08Decisions

Key design decisions

Typography first

Building the headline before showing products creates a calm, luxurious rhythm.

Plum and blush palette

A deep plum with blush pink feels premium and feminine without being loud.

Promo as the payoff

The animation ends on a product with a price, so beauty leads straight to buying.

09Design system

Visual language

Blush backgrounds, deep plum type, condensed bold headlines and a violet accent for the logo and buttons.

Plum#3B0B22
Blush#F2E1DA
