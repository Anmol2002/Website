01Overview

A store for nail professionals that also sells training courses, builds a community and rewards loyal customers.

NailSkillz sells professional nail products and runs training courses for nail technicians. I designed the complete website: homepage, collections, product pages, course listings, blog and articles, inspiration gallery, loyalty membership, business partner page, certified technician finder, cart and more.

15+page templates
3loyalty levels
2businesses in one site: shop and academy
1mobile version

02The challenge

What had to be solved

The brand has two audiences: professionals buying supplies and learners buying courses. Mixing both on one site can confuse both groups, and the product range (gels, acrylics, tools, accessories) is large.

Design goals

  1. Make the store and academy feel like one brand
  2. Help professionals find products fast
  3. Turn content and inspiration into sales
  4. Reward repeat buyers with a clear loyalty programme

03Process

How I approached it

  1. Two clear pathsShop and Training get their own navigation and landing pages, sharing one visual language.
  2. Category-first browsingCollection pages start with icon categories and filters suited to nail products.
  3. Content that sellsBlog, inspiration and articles link straight to the products they mention.
  4. Loyalty and partnersMembership levels and a business partner page support repeat and wholesale buyers.

04Homepage

Two businesses, one welcoming homepage.

A bold hero for the academy, promotional banners for the shop, bestsellers and community highlights.

05Collections and product page

Find the right gel in seconds.

Icon categories, product-specific filters and quick add. The product page shows shades, details, reviews and similar products.

06Training courses

Become a professional nail stylist.

Course listings with filters by format and level, and course pages with clear outcomes and pricing.

07Blog, articles and inspiration

Content that leads to products.

The blog, long-form articles and an inspiration gallery connect techniques to the products used.

08Membership, partners and cart

Reasons to come back.

Three loyalty levels with points rules, a business partner programme, a certified technician map and a clear cart with order summary.

09Mobile version

Built for professionals on the go.

The key pages adapted for mobile shopping.

10Decisions

Key design decisions

Separate navigation for shop and academy

Professionals and learners each get a direct route, so neither has to wade through the other's content.

Pink as an accent, not a background

The brand pink highlights actions and promotions, while product photos stay on clean white.

Loyalty shown in simple numbers

Points per euro and three levels are explained in one row, so the programme is understood at a glance.

11Design system

Visual language

Soft pink accents on white and black, rounded cards and product photography on clean backgrounds. A black footer anchors every page.

Blossom#E05A9B
Petal#F7D6E6
Ink#111111
White#FFFFFF

TypeClean sans-serif, bold headings

12Outcome

What was delivered

NailSkillz brings a store and an academy together in one website with more than 15 templates, a loyalty programme and content that links to products.

If the project continues, next I would:

  • Add course bundles with product kits
  • Design a reorder flow for professionals
  • Test filters with nail technicians
