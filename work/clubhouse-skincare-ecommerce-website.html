01Overview

An editorial skincare store where ingredients and texture do the selling.

Clubhouse Skin is a men's skincare brand with three hero products: a face cleanser, a vitamin C face oil and a moisturiser. I designed the homepage, the product pages and the mobile experience, with an editorial look built on bold photography and clear ingredient stories.

02The challenge

What had to be solved

Skincare shoppers compare ingredients and claims, but premium brands also need mood and story. Too much copy kills the mood; too little loses the sale.

Design goals

  1. Lead with brand mood on the homepage
  2. Explain ingredients in short, scannable blocks
  3. Keep add to bag visible near the product
  4. Work just as well on mobile

03Process

How I approached it

  1. Editorial homepageA full-bleed lifestyle image with the product line as a typographic list.
  2. Ingredient stripIcons for cactus extract, sea salt, squalene and vitamin C, each with one line.
  3. Texture momentsFull-width texture photography with the key benefit in large type.
  4. Mobile firstHero, product and ingredients stacked for one-handed scrolling.

04Homepage

Mood first, products always in reach.

An editorial hero with the full product line, followed by product stories and texture photography.

05Product pages

Ingredients that tell the story.

Product image, short description and add to bag at the top, then an ingredient strip, a texture banner and related products.

06Product art direction

Three products, one look.

Consistent product renders on deep green backgrounds tie the range together.

07Mobile

The same calm, in your hand.

The mobile hero and product page keep the editorial feel with thumb-friendly actions.

08Decisions

Key design decisions

Typography as navigation

The homepage hero lists the products in large type, so the brand image and the product menu are the same thing.

Short ingredient lines

Each ingredient gets an icon and one sentence, which keeps the page readable.

Deep green product backdrop

A consistent backdrop makes three different bottles look like one family.

09Design system

Visual language

Deep forest green, warm off-white and black, condensed uppercase headlines and a script signature for the brand mark.

10Outcome

What was delivered

Clubhouse Skin gets an editorial store where mood and ingredients work together, with product pages that explain the formula in seconds.
