01Overview

A calm, photo-led landing page that helps beginners and regulars pick the right yoga class and book a session.

Tree Yoga is a yoga and wellness studio offering beginner classes, flexibility work and stress relief sessions. I designed the landing page: a full-bleed calm hero, a short introduction, three programme cards, the teaching team, a wellness partner block, client reviews, a booking call to action, an FAQ and the footer.

9sections on one page
3programmes explained
5FAQ answers
1clear booking action

02The challenge

What had to be solved

Many people want to try yoga but feel they are not flexible or experienced enough. The page had to feel calm and welcoming, show there is a class for every level and make booking the obvious next step.

Design goals

  1. Set a calm mood from the first screen
  2. Show beginners there is a class for them
  3. Introduce the teachers as real people
  4. Make booking a session easy to find

03Process

How I approached it

  1. Calm heroA full-bleed meditation photo with the headline "Find your calm with every session" and one Start today button.
  2. Programmes by goalBeginner yoga, flexibility and stretching, and stress relief, each in one card.
  3. Real peopleTeacher photos and a personalised support card with Book a session.
  4. Reassure, then bookReviews, a booking block and an FAQ that answers the beginner questions.

04Hero

Calm from the first screen.

A warm, softly lit meditation photo fills the screen behind the headline, a two-line promise and a single Start today button.

05Introduction

Explain the benefit in plain words.

A short paragraph on feeling more active, less stressed and more connected, paired with two calm practice photos.

06Programmes

Yoga for every body and every lifestyle.

Three cards for beginner yoga, flexibility and stretching, and stress relief, with the last one in the dark olive accent to add rhythm.

07Team

Meet the people who teach.

Teacher portraits sit beside a personalised support card that explains how the studio helps each person choose a routine, with Book a session.

08Wellness partner

An everyday wellness partner.

A dark olive block with a forest walkway photo positions the studio as a long-term habit, not a one-off class.

09Reviews

200+ reviews, in short quotes.

Four review cards with names, handles, a short quote and a tag such as "Beginner friendly" or "20 min daily routine".

10Booking and FAQ

Start the journey, answer the doubts.

A studio class photo beside "Let's start your yoga journey" with Book a session, followed by five FAQ questions about experience, practising at home, clothing, plans and frequency.

11Footer

Everything else, neatly in one place.

An olive footer with quick links, programmes, about links and contact details.

12Decisions

Key design decisions

One accent colour

A muted olive is the only strong colour, so photos carry the warmth and buttons stay easy to spot.

Programmes by goal, not by style

Beginners understand "stress relief" faster than the names of yoga styles.

Soft leaf shapes

A faint leaf motif in card corners adds calm detail without competing with the photos.

13Design system

Visual language

Muted olive and sage with warm, natural photography, soft rounded cards, a friendly geometric sans and white pill buttons on dark blocks.

TypeFriendly geometric sans

14Outcome

What was delivered

Tree Yoga gets a calm landing page that welcomes beginners, explains each programme in one card, introduces the teachers and keeps "Book a session" within reach on every screen.

If the project continues, next I would:

  • Add a class timetable with live availability
  • Design an online booking and payment flow
  • Create a members area with recorded sessions
