01Overview

From skill assessment to job offers: an AI app that shows where you stand and what to do next.

CareerPilot AI helps professionals plan their next career move. It assesses skills, gives a career score, builds a learning roadmap, recommends jobs and helps improve a resume and practise interviews. I designed all 30 screens, from onboarding to subscription.

30screens designed
3-stepprofile setup
6AI tools in one app
78/100career score at a glance

02The challenge

What had to be solved

Career advice is usually scattered across job boards, courses and resume tools. Users don't know which skills matter for the role they want, or whether they are ready to apply.

Design goals

  1. Show users exactly where they stand against a target role
  2. Turn gaps into a realistic weekly plan
  3. Connect learning, jobs, resume and interviews in one journey
  4. Make AI results feel explainable, not magic

03Process

How I approached it

  1. Profile in three stepsAbout you, skill self-assessment and career goals, each with progress shown.
  2. One career scoreA single score with trend becomes the anchor for every other feature.
  3. Explain the AIInsights, skill gaps and resume feedback always show the evidence behind them.
  4. Close the loopJob details link to resume improvements and interview practice for that role.

04Onboarding and login

Your next chapter, with direction.

Three onboarding screens explain discovery, progress tracking and personal AI guidance. Login stays simple with a clear brand mark.

05Profile and skill assessment

Three steps to a personal plan.

Profile, skill ratings and goals are a three-step flow. An AI processing screen sets expectations before the first results appear.

06Insights and roadmap

Where you stand, and how to close the gap.

Career insights list strengths and weaknesses with evidence. Skill gap analysis compares the user against the target role, and the roadmap breaks it into monthly milestones.

07Jobs and resume

Matches you can act on.

Job recommendations show a match score and why. Resume upload leads to a scored analysis and before-and-after improvements written by AI.

08Interview practice and tracking

Practise, then track every application.

AI interview sessions end with scored feedback. The application tracker, saved jobs and achievements keep momentum going.

09Plans, profile and settings

The supporting screens, done properly.

Subscription plans, profile and settings reuse the same cards and green accents.

10Decisions

Key design decisions

A single score as the anchor

The career score gives every feature a shared measure, so users can see whether learning or resume edits are moving them forward.

Evidence next to every AI claim

Strengths, gaps and resume feedback all show the reason, which makes AI results feel trustworthy.

Role-led, not feature-led

Everything is organised around the target role the user chose, not around separate tools.

11Design system

Visual language

Fresh green for progress and positive signals, mint surfaces, dark ink text. Progress bars, score rings and match chips are reusable components.

12Outcome

What was delivered

CareerPilot AI connects assessment, learning, jobs, resume and interviews into one guided journey. Each AI feature explains itself, and the career score ties progress together.

If the project continues, next I would:

  • Add mentor booking
  • Test match explanations with job seekers
  • Design a web dashboard version
