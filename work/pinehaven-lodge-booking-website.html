01Overview

A booking website for forest lodges that sells the feeling first, then makes booking simple.

Pinehaven rents handpicked lodges in forests and by lakes. I designed the full website: homepage, lodge finder, lodge detail, booking, experiences, journal and about pages, plus the motion variants used for entrance animations.

7pages designed
1-pagebooking flow
120+lodges in the content model
1motion system

02The challenge

What had to be solved

Booking sites usually feel like spreadsheets of rooms. Pinehaven's guests are buying calm, so the site had to feel slow and beautiful while still answering price, dates and availability quickly.

Design goals

  1. Sell the atmosphere on the first screen
  2. Make finding and comparing lodges easy
  3. Keep booking to one page
  4. Add story through experiences and a journal

03Process

How I approached it

  1. Atmosphere firstFull-bleed dusk photography and an elegant serif set the tone.
  2. Find your forestLodge cards with price, location and filters for region and type.
  3. Booking on one pageDates, guests, extras and payment with a sticky summary.
  4. Motion with restraintSoft entrance animations designed as variants in Figma.

04Homepage

Sell the quiet, then show the lodges.

A cinematic hero, the brand promise, key numbers and this season's favourite lodges.

05Lodge finder

Find your forest.

A grid of lodges with filters, price and location, so comparing feels easy.

06Lodge detail

Everything about one stay.

Gallery, host, amenities, house rules and a booking card that follows the scroll.

07Booking

Confirm your stay on one page.

Dates, guests, extras and payment with a clear price summary.

08Experiences, journal and about

Do less. Notice more.

Experiences, journal stories and the brand's origin turn a booking site into a place people return to.

09Motion

Entrance animations, designed as variants.

Each animated element was designed as Figma variants for a smooth handoff to development.

10Decisions

Key design decisions

Serif for feeling, sans for facts

Headlines use an elegant serif while prices, dates and buttons use a clear sans-serif.

Sticky booking card

On the lodge page the price and dates stay visible, so the decision never scrolls away.

Warm amber accent

One warm accent against forest greens draws the eye to actions without breaking the mood.

11Design system

Visual language

Forest green and warm cream with an amber accent, cinematic photography and an elegant serif paired with a neutral sans-serif.

Amber#C8813A
Forest#1E2A22
Cream#F3EEE4
Moss#5C6B4E

TypeElegant serif headlines

12Outcome

What was delivered

Pinehaven gets a calm, cinematic booking website with seven pages, a one-page booking flow and a motion system ready for development.

If the project continues, next I would:

  • Add a map view of lodges
  • Design gift vouchers
  • Test booking on mobile
