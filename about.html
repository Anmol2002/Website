MY JOURNEY · STORY MODE

HOW I GOT HERE

Scroll to play the story

01/ 08Foundation

Pixels before purpose

It started with a Bachelor’s in Graphics & Web Designing at Guru Nanak Dev University: layout, type, colour and my first websites.

Plot twistGood-looking screens were not always screens people could use.

Level upDesign has to work, not just look nice.

02/ 08Certified

Learning to ask why

The Google UX Design Professional Certificate taught me the structured way: user research, wireframes, prototypes and testing.

Plot twistJumping straight to the UI felt faster, but it hid the real problems.

Level upFlows and wireframes come before pixels.

03/ 082021

Zero reviews, a world going online

Businesses everywhere were moving online, and I started freelancing on Upwork with Shopify and WooCommerce stores and landing pages.

Plot twistA brand-new profile, no reviews and designers from every country bidding for the same jobs.

Level upReply fast, deliver on time, and let the work speak.

04/ 08Worldwide

Different time zones, one workflow

Clients came from across the world while I worked from Mohali, India.

Plot twistFeedback arriving while I slept, and no time to repeat a call.

Level upOrganised Figma files, clickable prototypes and clear written updates.

05/ 08Top Rated

Trust, earned one project at a time

Project by project, the reviews added up: Top Rated on Upwork, 100% Job Success, a 5.0 average rating and 1,200+ hours delivered.

Plot twistEvery new client still has to trust someone they have never met.

Level upConsistency is the best sales pitch.

06/ 08AI arrives

AI changed the brief

AI showed up in every product. Clients asked for AI agents, AI money coaches and AI career tools, not just screens.

Plot twistDesigning for features that can be wrong, and users who are not sure they trust them yet.

Level upShow what the AI did and why, and keep people in control.

07/ 082026

Levelling up again

Advanced UI/UX Design & Product Experience: deeper product thinking for SaaS, AI tools and mobile apps.

Plot twistTools and trends change every few months.

Level upKeep learning, keep the user at the centre.

08/ 08Today

The next chapter could be yours

Today I design mobile apps, SaaS platforms, e-commerce stores, landing pages and motion for clients worldwide.
