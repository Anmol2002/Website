Who I am

This website is run by Manpreet Kaur, a freelance UI/UX designer based in Mohali, Punjab, India (“I”, “me”). I am responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact me at any time at uiuxflow19@gmail.com.

What information I collect

I only collect information you choose to send me:

  • Contact and partnership forms: your name, email address and message, plus anything else you fill in, such as company, website, the service you need, budget or monthly hours.
  • Emails: anything you send to me directly, including attachments.
  • Call bookings: if you book a free call, Google Calendar’s appointment scheduling collects your name, email and the time you choose.

Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may automatically log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels. If that changes, I will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

How I use your information

  • To reply to your message and answer your questions
  • To prepare quotes and proposals you ask for
  • To schedule and hold calls you book
  • To deliver and manage projects we agree to work on
  • To keep basic records of our communication

I do not sell your personal data, share it with advertisers or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Services that process your data

A few trusted services help me run this website and my work. They only process your data to provide their service:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form messages to my inbox
  • Google (Gmail and Google Calendar) for email and call bookings
  • My website hosting provider to serve this website
  • Upwork, if you choose to contact or hire me there, under Upwork’s own privacy policy

Some of these services may store data on servers outside India.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to reply and follow up, and for up to 24 months afterwards in case you get in touch again. Project records may be kept longer where needed for accounting or legal reasons. You can ask me to delete your data sooner at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for visitors in the EU or UK, the GDPR, you can:

  • Ask what personal data I hold about you
  • Ask me to correct or update it
  • Ask me to delete it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Raise a concern or complaint

To use any of these rights, email uiuxflow19@gmail.com. I will reply as soon as possible and within 30 days.

Keeping your data safe

I use reputable services with secure connections and keep access to your information limited to me. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information through the forms.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites such as Upwork, Behance, LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact and grievances

If you have any questions or concerns about your personal data, contact Manpreet Kaur at uiuxflow19@gmail.com.
