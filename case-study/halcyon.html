HALCYONFootwear e-commerce · self-initiated concept

Weightless, by design. - sneaker e-commerce website UI/UX case study

A premium sneaker store where one hero product does all the talking - built to sell a feeling, not a spec sheet.

Industry
E-commerce
Type
Self-initiated concept
Role
UI/UX · visual · motion
Deliverables
Website, animated hero, design system
01The brief02Goals03User flow04Colourways05Product page06Design system07Outcome

01The brief

Sell a feeling, not a spec sheet.

HALCYON is a limited sneaker drop - 2,700 numbered pairs of the HX-01, with a visible four-chamber air sole. The store needed to feel as light as the product and make that sole impossible to miss.

Most sneaker sites bury the product under banners and grids. I designed a single-product experience: one hero, three colourways, every angle and a short path to the bag.

02Goals

Three things it had to do.

Make the product feel weightlessA dark stage, warm light and a floating shoe put the amber air sole front and centre.
Show every angle, no 3D modelSide, heel and sole views plus tech hotspots explain the build without heavy 3D.
Shorten the path to bagColourway, size and “Add to bag” live on one screen; the bag opens as a drawer.

03User flow

From first look to bag, on one page.

Each step answers the question a shopper is asking at that moment. Average path: six steps, all on one page - no category grids, no detours.

  1. Land“What is this?”
  2. Colourway“Which one is me?”
  3. Angles“Show me more.”
  4. Tech“Why is it special?”
  5. Size“Does it fit?”
  6. Bag“I want it.”

04Colourways

Three moods, one layout.

Solar, Obsidian and Glacier change the mood of the whole page while the layout stays the same, so switching never feels like leaving the product.

05Product page

Size, colourway and Add to bag on one screen.

Everything a buyer needs to decide sits together. The bag opens as a drawer, so adding a pair never breaks the mood.

06Design system

Swap one accent token and the store re-themes.

Colour tokens, type and components are built so each colourway is a token change, not a redesign.

07Outcome

One product, told beautifully.

A product-first stageOne hero product, lit and framed so the air sole is impossible to miss.
A six-step pathFrom first look to bag without leaving the page.
Themeable by designThree colourways from one token swap.
