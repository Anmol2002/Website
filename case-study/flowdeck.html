FLOWDECKAI SaaS platform · concept product

Make ops work move itself. - AI SaaS platform dashboard UI/UX case study

FLOWDECK is an AI workspace that reads every request and routes it to the right person or AI agent - with live SLAs and insights in one calm place.

SaaS

01The brief

Requests arrive everywhere. Ownership arrives nowhere.

Ops teams get requests from email, Slack, forms and a dozen tools. Someone has to read each one, find the owner and chase it - and SLAs slip.

What had to change

Too many channelsEmail, chat, forms and tools each hold part of the queue.
Manual triageA person reads every request just to decide who owns it.
Invisible riskSLA breaches are only noticed once a customer complains.
The design question

How might one workspace let AI do the sorting while people stay in charge of what matters?

02The approach

AI tags and routes. Agents handle the repeatable. People approve.

I designed one calm workspace around three jobs: capture every request in one queue, let AI and agents move routine work forward and keep humans in the loop for decisions.

  1. CaptureEvery channel lands in one inbox.
  2. UnderstandAI suggests intent, owner and next action.
  3. RouteWorkflows send work to a person or an AI agent.
  4. ApproveHumans sign off where judgment matters.
  5. MeasureLive insights show response times and SLA risk.

03Workflows dashboard

KPIs, progress and status at a glance.

The dashboard is where a lead starts the day: what is moving, what is stuck and which workflows need attention.

04Unified inbox

Every channel in one queue.

Requests from every source sit in one list. An AI panel beside each request suggests intent, owner and a draft reply, so the next action is one click away.

Suggest, don’t decideAI proposes the owner and reply; a person accepts or edits.
Context in placeCustomer, channel and history sit next to the message.
Actions, not tabsAssign, reply and escalate from the same screen.

05Workflow builder

Automation without code, with a human in the loop.

A visual builder chains triggers, conditions, AI agents and human approvals. Teams can read a workflow like a sentence and change it without an engineer.

06Live insights

Fix problems before customers notice.

Response times, channel mix and team workload are shown together, so leads can spot SLA risk and rebalance work early.

07Mobile

The queue, in your pocket.

Six key screens bring the inbox, approvals and alerts to mobile, so a lead can unblock work between meetings.

08Design system

Tokens and live components behind every screen.

Web app, mobile and website share one set of colour, type and spacing tokens and a component library, so new screens stay consistent.

09Outcome

One calm workspace for every request.

One queueEvery channel in one inbox, with AI suggestions on each request.
Automation you can readVisual workflows with AI agents and human approvals.
Visible SLAsInsights that show risk before it becomes a complaint.
One systemWeb, mobile and website on shared tokens and components.
