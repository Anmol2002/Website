CASE STUDY - AI PERSONAL FINANCE APP

MONEO - AI personal finance app UI/UX case study

An AI money coach that finds leaks in your spending and turns them into goals you actually reach.

INDUSTRY

Personal Finance

TYPE

Concept product

ROLE

UX research · UI · Prototype

PLATFORM

iOS & Android

THE BRIEF

MONEY APPS SHOW DATA.
PEOPLE NEED DIRECTION.

Most finance apps stop at charts. People see where money went, feel bad and close the app - nothing changes next month.

MONEO adds an AI coach on top: it spots subscription and spending leaks, explains them in plain words and moves the saved money straight into a goal.

8core screens
4wireframes before UI
3taps to move money to a goal
1token-based design system

KEY FEATURES

01

AI money coach

Chat-style coach that answers ‘can I afford this?’ with your real numbers.

02

Leak finder

Flags unused subscriptions and spending spikes, with one-tap fixes.

03

Goals that fill themselves

Saved money flows into goals, with progress you can feel.
