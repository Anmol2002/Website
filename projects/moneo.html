MONEOAI personal finance app · concept product

Money apps show data. People need direction. - AI personal finance app UI/UX case study

MONEO is an AI money coach that finds leaks in your spending and turns them into goals you actually reach.

Industry
Personal finance
Type
Concept product
Role
UX research · UI · prototype
Platform
iOS and Android
01The brief02Key features03User flow04Wireframe to final05Final screens06Design system07Outcome

01The brief

Charts don’t change habits.

Most finance apps stop at charts. People see where money went, feel bad and close the app - nothing changes next month. MONEO adds an AI coach on top: it spots subscription and spending leaks, explains them in plain words and moves the saved money straight into a goal.
