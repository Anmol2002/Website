01The product

Six financial worlds. One mobile product.

Metapixel is a mobile SaaS product for a crypto and digital-finance platform. It brings banking, cards, crypto wallets, transfers, check deposits, contacts and transaction history into a single app.

Banking
Accounts, transfers, check deposits and account activity.
Cards
Physical and virtual cards with balances and spending limits.
Crypto
Wallets, assets and buy, sell, send and receive journeys.
Payments
Contacts, sending funds and transaction history.
Identity
Sign up, personal details, account setup and KYC.
Product layer
Dashboard, referrals, education and supporting states.

02The design challenge

Each feature was simple. Together, they weren’t.

Every flow carried its own information, decisions, validation and status. The job wasn’t to produce polished screens - it was to make a large financial ecosystem feel understandable and predictable.

Many journeysUsers move between onboarding, banking, cards, crypto, contacts and transactions.
High-trust actionsTransfers, deposits and identity flows need clarity before and after the action.
Different user statesNew users need guidance; returning users need fast access to everyday actions.
Large surface200+ screens demanded reusable patterns, not one-off UI decisions.
The design question

How can a complex financial ecosystem feel simple enough to use every day?

  1. GuideUsers always know what happens next.
  2. SimplifyComplex actions break into understandable steps.
  3. ReassureImportant actions get clear confirmation and status.
  4. ConnectBanking, crypto, cards and payments feel like one product.

03Onboarding and trust

Earn trust before asking for data.

Before anyone moves money, they need to trust the product. Entry is broken into short, legible steps instead of one long form - each screen asks for one kind of information and explains itself where doubt is likely.

Login›Sign up›Personal details›Account checklist›KYC›Dashboard
  1. One entry, one choiceLogin is the primary action; sign-up sits directly beneath it.
  2. Validated as you goEach field confirms itself and consent is explicit before the account exists.
  3. Progress made visibleA two-step stepper - Personal Info → Address Info - sets expectations upfront.
  4. Context at the point of doubt“Why are we taking your mobile number?” sits beside the field it explains.

04Dashboard and information architecture

One home screen, shaped by who’s arriving.

The dashboard is the starting point for every journey. It adapts to where the user is - setup for new users, everyday actions for returning ones and a dedicated state once crypto wallets are active.

The card is the interface.

Physical and virtual cards share one visual language. The card is the visual anchor; available balance, label and spending limit sit directly beneath it.

Card → Balance → Spending limit → Details → Add card
Users move from recognition to action without searching.

05High-trust financial flows

Slow down the moments that move money.

Transfers and deposits are high-intent, high-anxiety actions. Every decision gets its own labelled space and verification and confirmation are explicit steps - never implied.

Choose the routeContact, intrabank or another bank.
One decision per fieldSource, destination, amount, description.
Verify before it movesFace ID gates the transfer.
Confirmation restates itAmount, source, destination, description.

06Web3 experience

Crypto, explained through familiar actions.

Wallets, assets and addresses are unfamiliar concepts. The experience is organised around everyday verbs, so people can start without understanding the whole system first.

Create›Discover›Manage›Transact›Track

Same patterns, new domain.

Crypto reuses the dashboard’s cards, lists and education modules, so moving between fiat and Web3 never feels like switching apps.

07Contacts and supporting states

People are the shortest path to a payment.

Contacts aren’t an address book bolted on. Each contact is a starting point for financial action - identity, details, transactions and Send funds live in one place.

Empty
No contacts, cards or wallets yet
Success
Transfer, account, contact created
Verification
Under review, docs required
Correction
Missing info, retake flows
Confirmation
Before and after high-trust actions
Loading
Feedback while work happens

At this scale, consistency can’t depend on checking every frame by hand. Reusable components and shared interaction patterns carry the product from one flow to the next.
