Turning career uncertainty into a guided path. - AI career coach mobile app UI/UX case study

A mobile career coach that helps people understand their strengths, see their skill gaps, follow a learning plan, improve their resume, practise interviews and find relevant jobs - in one connected journey.

01The product

One career journey, from self-discovery to the interview.

CareerPilot AI brings discovery, assessment, coaching, learning, job search and interview preparation into a single mobile experience.

  1. DiscoverCareer direction and onboarding
  2. AssessSkills, confidence and goals
  3. GuideAI insights and next steps
  4. GrowSkill gaps and a learning roadmap
  5. ApplyJobs, resume and applications
  6. PracticeAI interview preparation

02The design challenge

Career decisions are complex. The next step shouldn’t be.

People move through many moments - understanding themselves, choosing a direction, closing gaps, applying - and need to keep moving after each one.

What made it hard

Every feature had to feel like the next step of the same journey, not another tool to learn.

Too many decisions
Direction feels vague without a starting point.
Personal by default
It must adapt to goals, skills and progress.
Insight → action
Insights matter once they become next steps.
Continuity
Jobs, resume and interviews should feel connected.
The design question

How might we turn career uncertainty into a personalized, actionable path?

Design principles

PersonalizeProfile, skills and goals feed every recommendation.
GuideEach screen leads to one clear next action.
ExplainAI feedback says what to change, why and what is next.
MotivateScores, streaks and milestones make progress visible.

03Onboarding and personalization

A guided start, not a form.

Advice is only useful with context. Onboarding gathers it in three short, focused steps - then hands the user straight to the analysis.

Welcome›Create account›Profile›Skills›Goals›AI analysis
  1. Progress you can see“Step 1 of 3” and a progress bar set expectations from the very first field.
  2. Rate, don’t writeOne tap per skill on a 1-5 scale. “Update these anytime” keeps it low-stakes.
  3. Goals become inputsUp to three goals, picked as cards, shape the roadmap and matches that follow.

Collect just enough to personalize - and keep the user moving toward value.

Login, account creation and password recovery stay single-purpose: one primary action per screen, with inline validation.

04Career insights

Turning career data into clear next steps.

The analysis builds anticipation; the dashboard and insights turn the result into a personal snapshot - recommendations, not raw data.

Profile + goals›Analysis›Snapshot›Insights›Next action
  1. Show the workNamed steps - skills mapped, market matched - replace a blank spinner.
  2. Snapshot firstCareer score, resume score and readiness sit above the fold.
  3. Explain the whyStrengths and weaknesses cite the scores behind them.
  4. One tap to actResume, interview, jobs and learning start from home.

The system doesn’t stop at “you need this skill.” It compares today with the target role, ranks what matters most and turns it into a plan.

06Job discovery

Making the right opportunity easier to recognize.

Job search is framed as the next step in the career plan - not a separate destination. Recommendations speak the same language of fit as the rest of the product.

Discover›Filter›Evaluate›Save or apply

07Resume and interview coaching

Feedback that explains what to change and why.

Resume support closes the gap between career insight and application quality: understand the score, apply targeted edits, export a better version.

08States and UI system

Consistency turns many features into one product.

Every flow is built from the same small set of components - and every action has a visible state, so users always know what just happened.
