TravellerMobile product design

A smarter way to discover, plan and experience a trip. - travel trip planner mobile app UI/UX case study

A mobile travel experience that brings discovery, itinerary planning, travel utilities and group coordination into one connected journey.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
Mobile app
Focus
Product design · UX · UI · prototyping

01The challenge

Travel planning shouldn’t feel like another project.

Too many disconnected toolsMaps, booking platforms, notes, weather apps and expense tools - all open at once.
Too much informationOptions, prices and logistics pile up quickly. Planning becomes overwhelming.
Hard to keep it togetherIdeas, activities, budgets and people end up scattered across different places.

02The UX idea

One trip. One connected experience.

Every feature answers the same question: what does this trip need next?

  1. DiscoverFind destinations and experiences worth exploring.
  2. PlanTurn ideas into a structured itinerary.
  3. PrepareKeep logistics, budget and travel utilities together.
  4. Travel togetherCoordinate plans with the people you’re travelling with.

03Discover

Start with inspiration.

Rich imagery first, useful detail second - browse casually, zoom in when ready to decide.

  1. Visual discoveryRich imagery helps users scan destinations quickly.
  2. Easy explorationClear categories and search reduce friction.
  3. Decision-ready detailsWeather, ideal stay and price range - right where the choice happens.

04Plan the trip

Turn ideas into an itinerary.

The itinerary acts as the single source of truth for the trip.

Every other feature hangs off it, so nothing has to be copied between tools.
  1. Create tripName, place, dates and travellers.
  2. Trip overviewStays, transport, group and budget at a glance.
  3. TimelineEvery day, in order, readable at a glance.
  4. Day planMorning, afternoon and evening blocks.
  5. Add activityAdded from inside the plan, not a separate tool.

05The travel toolkit

Everything you need once the trip gets real.

Bookings, money and travel utilities live inside the same trip - so practical decisions stay connected to the plan.

Book
Flights and stays
Manage
Budget and expenses
Prepare
Packing, documents, weather and currency

06Travel together

Because great trips are rarely solo.

Members, conversation and updates sit right next to the itinerary - the social side of travel isn’t another disconnected tool.

Shared itinerary
The four travellers of “The Aegean crew”, linked to the same plan.
Group expenses
Who paid what, inside the trip.
Group chat
Updates and documents next to the plan.

Planning together should feel as easy as travelling together.

From scattered planning to one clear journey.
