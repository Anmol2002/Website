CASE STUDY - FOOTWEAR E-COMMERCE

HALCYON - sneaker e-commerce website UI/UX case study

Weightless, by design - a premium sneaker store where one hero product does all the talking.

INDUSTRY

E-commerce

TYPE

Self-initiated concept

ROLE

UI/UX · Visual · Motion

DELIVERABLES

Website, animated hero, design system

THE BRIEF

SELL A FEELING,
NOT A SPEC SHEET.

HALCYON is a limited sneaker drop - 2,700 numbered pairs of the HX-01, with a visible four-chamber air sole. The store needed to feel as light as the product and make that sole impossible to miss.

Most sneaker sites bury the product under banners and grids. I designed a single-product experience: one hero, three colourways, every angle and a short path to the bag.

12Screens designed
3Colourways
5Micro-interactions
1Design system

GOALS

THREE THINGS IT HAD TO DO

01

Make the product feel weightless

A dark stage, warm light and a floating shoe put the amber air sole front and centre.

02

Show every angle, no 3D model

Side, heel and sole views plus tech hotspots explain the build without heavy 3D.

03

Shorten the path to bag

Colourway, size and “Add to bag” live on one screen; the bag opens as a drawer.
