CASE STUDY - HOSPITALITY WEBSITE

PINEHAVEN - hospitality lodge booking website UI/UX case study

A calm, nature-first website for booking cabins and lodges.

INDUSTRY

Hospitality

PLATFORM

Website · 8 pages

ROLE

UI/UX · Visual design

PAGES

Home, Lodges, Detail, Booking, Experiences, Journal, About, Sign in

THE BRIEF

BOOK THE QUIET,
NOT THE HASSLE.

Lodge sites often bury availability under long pages and pop-ups, so guests leave before they book.

I designed a slow, photo-led experience with a clear path: browse lodges, see the details, pick dates and book - with experiences and stories to inspire the stay.

KEY FEATURES

01

Lodge finder

Filters for dates, guests and stay type with a visual results grid.

02

Lodge detail

Gallery, amenities, reviews and a sticky booking card.

03

Experiences

Canoeing, campfires and trails that sell the stay, not just the room.
